# Brand guidelines (one-pager)

## Mark

- **Primary symbol:** `assets/icon.svg` (512×512)
- **README hero:** `assets/logo.svg` + `assets/logo-animated.svg`

## Color

All fills come from the application theme ([references/token-table.md](../references/token-table.md)). Do not invent hex values for UI surfaces.

## Clear space

Minimum padding around the squircle mark: 8% of icon width.

## Don't

- Trace marks from `library/` (study only).
- Ship wordmark-only banners without the 3-column blueprint.
